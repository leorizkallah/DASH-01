Quiet-pose recovery: inspect the best policies

Each 20-second video compares four fixed test cases: quiet reference (top left), moderate deviation (top right), larger deviation (bottom left), hard deviation (bottom right). There are no external pushes. Left foot is blue; right foot is orange.

Angles label the requested reset noise before physical projection. Each is a different random pose and direction, not a scaled version of another panel. A nominally larger sample can be easier for the policy. Actual initial motor deviations are shown.

The video PASS/FAIL label is the full 20-second retention outcome. A robot can complete its initial five-second hold and still fail if it drifts later; the table separates these two results. A terminated episode freezes its last valid pose with a label; no restart is spliced into the clip.
